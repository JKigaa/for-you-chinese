Our Story

A converted house that became a Nairobi habit.

For You began as a family kitchen in a converted house on Gitanga Road and never lost that feeling. There are no sharp edges here, justwarm wood, red curtains, beige tablecloths and the sound of a full room on a Sunday afternoon.

The cooking is straightforward Chinese done in quantity: handmade dumplings, sizzling platters, whole fried fish, and mountains of fried rice meant to be pushed around a turntable and shared. It is the kind of menu where ordering too much is the correct decision.

“You can relax while you eat while the little ones are kept entertained.”

Outside there is a proper playground with an attendant on duty at weekends, and every child leaves with a complimentary ice cream. Groups get private rooms. Everyone gets a table that can actually hold the food.

Family-style cooking

Every dish is built for the middle of the table. Order wide, share everything.

A playground for the kids

Supervised play area, free ice cream, and room to run while lunch happens.

Private dining rooms

Separate rooms for birthdays, family gatherings and long catch-ups.

Genuinely good value

Most plates sit between KSh 300 and 1,900, and they arrive very full.
